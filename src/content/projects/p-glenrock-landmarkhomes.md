# Glenrock  
## Modern Desert Living in Summerlin West

Glenrock by Landmark Homes is a premier residential community located in the growing Summerlin West district of Las Vegas, Nevada. Designed to blend modern luxury with natural serenity, Glenrock offers thoughtfully crafted one- and two-story homes with cutting-edge sustainability features, smart technology, and access to world-class amenities.

Situated just 20 minutes from the Las Vegas Strip and adjacent to the breathtaking Red Rock Canyon, Glenrock combines the tranquility of suburban living with the vibrancy of urban life. Featuring 168 homes across 49 acres, the community is designed to appeal to families and professionals seeking elevated living in an energy-efficient, well-connected environment.

Developed by Landmark Homes’ Greater Nevada Division, Glenrock represents the 2023–2024 NAHB Student Competition entry from Purdue University.

## Project Overview

Glenrock sets a new benchmark for livable, sustainable design in the Southwest. With innovative floorplans, ENERGY STAR® certified construction, and a strong commitment to community engagement, the neighborhood balances comfort, performance, and lifestyle in a highly desirable desert setting.

### Key Highlights

- 168 thoughtfully designed homes across ~49 acres  
- Four flexible home models ranging from 2,000–2,800 sq ft  
- Mountain and skyline views throughout the community  
- Five landscaped pocket parks  
- Proximity to Red Rock Canyon and Downtown Summerlin  
- Zoned for top-rated Clark County schools  

## Location: Summerlin West, Las Vegas

Glenrock is located in Summerlin West, one of Las Vegas’s fastest-growing residential districts.

Residents enjoy:

- Direct access to Red Rock Canyon National Conservation Area  
- Close proximity to Downtown Summerlin shopping and dining  
- Easy access to UNLV and major employment hubs  
- A short commute to the Las Vegas Strip  

This location offers the rare balance of outdoor recreation, urban convenience, and peaceful neighborhood living.

## Sustainability & Performance

Glenrock homes are built with energy efficiency and environmental responsibility at their core.

### Sustainability Features

- ENERGY STAR® certified homes  
- HERS Index targets between 55–65  
- High-performance Low-E windows  
- Smart thermostats and LED lighting  
- Energy-efficient appliances  
- Water-saving plumbing fixtures  
- Advanced framing techniques  
- Locally sourced materials where possible  
- Blower Door and Duct Blaster testing for performance verification  
- Up to 50% estimated energy cost savings  

Together, these strategies create homes that are healthier, more efficient, and built for long-term value.
